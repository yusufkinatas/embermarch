## Items
- **B1 fixed.** `BattleActions.attackAt`: an A-click on a visible enemy unit or a seen enemy building (owner ENEMY, not untargetable) issues `{type:'attack', targetId, queue: shift}` for every selected unit; anywhere else it stays an attack-move. Shift still queues (the direct attack becomes a queued waypoint). `targetAt(x, y, shift, allowTarget = true)`: the minimap passes false, so minimap A-clicks stay attack-moves. The Attack-Move tooltip now says "or click an enemy to attack it". Browser (1600x900, raze d1 seed 3, 4 Bowmen + 4 Ashpikes, A + click on the Nest): all 8 get `attack` on the Nest, its HP drops at +0.2 s (800 → 743 in 5 s), the Ashpikes stand 2-3 tiles from its centre and the Bowmen shoot from range. Tests: 4 in `tests/game/actions.test.ts`, plus `e2e/battle-touch.spec.ts` B1.
- **B2 fixed.** `input.ts`: touch with no placement/targeting mode: one finger dragging more than 10 px pans; a tap on an own unit or building selects it (double-tap selects the type); a tap anywhere else with own things selected runs `smartAt` (move, attack, gather, build, rally). Two fingers pinch-zoom around their midpoint (and pan with it). Touch never edge-pans; `pointercancel` and `reset()` clear touch state. Safari `gesturestart/change/end` are prevented at document level and `gesturechange` zooms the camera; Ctrl+wheel is prevented on the battle root (HUD panels included). `gesture()` (audio unlock) is also called on pointerup. `hudScale` goes down to 0.6 when min(w, h) < 500, and the HUD root gets a `phone` class below 0.85: command buttons with two or three costs then wrap their costs under a smaller icon (10 px on screen, three costs at 9.5 px as "50 35 / 20"); nothing clips. The coach card has a max-width that keeps it clear of the tracker in narrow windows (unchanged at 1280 and up). Browser: iPhone 15 landscape (WebKit, 734x343, scale 0.6, bottom panel 30% of the height, was 42%) and Galaxy A55 landscape (Chromium, 1040x480, 0.667, 24%): tap selects a Tiller, a tap 2 tiles away gives a `move` and it walks there, a 160 px drag pans 5 tiles with the selection kept and no box, the Counsel card and the tracker do not overlap (also with a wave on the Horn), a real CDP two-finger pinch zooms 1 → 1.5 with visualViewport scale 1. Synthetic gesture events in WebKit and Chromium: zoom 1 → 1.2 → 1.4, all prevented; Ctrl+wheel over the tracker prevented. Tests: 5 touch tests in `tests/game/input.test.ts`, phone scale in `tests/game/layout.test.ts`, `e2e/battle-touch.spec.ts` B2.
- **B3 fixed.** `UnattendedFoundations` counts sim-core's parked job (`ucore(world, u).savedOrder` / `savedQueue`, read-only) as attended, and a build queued behind an endless order (gather, hold) as not attended. With no saved order it models what `restoreSaved` resumes (the gather job, else idle). Tests: the 25 s Hearth Watch rally case (no notice in 30 s, builders return), a build queued behind a gather (notice at exactly 15 s), and flee-parked builds.
- **B4 fixed.** Boss bar phase label is 'Wrath' when `boss.wrath` (before Warded / Uprooted), with a `wrath` class (red border) and the tooltip "Wrath: the Regent tore free at 8:00. +20% damage, and the surviving Root Hearts keep sending waves." (built from `BOSS.wrathTime` / `wrathDamagePct`). While warded the tooltip adds "Wrath in m:ss". The tracker styles a segment starting with 'Wrath' amber and red under 1:00 (uses `boss.wrathIn` when present). The phase-2 callout reads "Wrath / The Regent tears free. +20% damage." when the Regent tears free in Wrath. The Spent ribbon passes 'Root Heart' in the boss battle. Browser (boss battle 1600x900): tracker "Burn the Root Hearts 0/3 · Wrath 8:00" amber, "Wrath 0:50" red at 7:10, bar 'Wrath' after 8:00, ribbon "Wave 6 broken. ENE Root Heart Spent 0:44".
- **B5 fixed.** Each ribbon remembers the wave, the battle time it broke and the last Spent time read. The wave number is dropped (giving "SW Nest Spent 1:18") once the timer jumps up (a later launch refreshed it) or 15 battle seconds after the break. `nestDir` prefers the view's `nests[i].dir` ('near SW' / 'far SW') and refreshes it each update. The edge-marker labels name no direction, so edgeArrows needed no change. Found and fixed during the browser check: before the view reports the Nest as Spent it reads 0 left, which looked like a jump and dropped the number at once (`spentReading`). Browser (raze d2 seed 23): "Wave 1 broken. near SW Nest Spent 0:49" and "Wave 2 broken. far SW Nest Spent 1:04", 16 s later "near SW Nest Spent 0:31" / "far SW Nest Spent 0:47". Tests: `tests/game/spent.test.ts`.
- **B6 fixed.** `statSources.structureNote` reads the composed `unitStats(...).structMod`: under ×0.5 → Weak vs building icon ("Barely damages buildings ×0.2"), from ×1 → Strong vs ("Tears down buildings ×4"), nothing between. Uses the existing `fort` glyph. Browser (WebKit): Bowman Weak vs shows cavalry + the building icon; Gatebreaker Strong vs shows the building icon. Tests: Bowman 0.2, Courser 0.4, Gatebreaker 4, Ashpike / Warden / Tiller none, Raider's Bounty (Courser ×0.8) none.
- **B7 fixed.** `BattleCamera.topBand` (CSS px) widens the top clamp for pans and zooms so map row 0 can come down to half a tile below the band. `centerOn` keeps the old clamp, so H, Space, group recall and the opening view frame exactly as before. The HUD measures the band as the bottom of the tracker column (card plus Boon and modifier rows) on layout and at 10 Hz, and reports it through the new `HudHooks.topBand`. Browser (raze d1 seed 13, NE Hearth, 1600x900): band 111 px (174 with 3 Boons and 2 modifiers), pan fully up-right puts row 0 at 127 (190), a right-click on the tree at (54, 2) hits the canvas and sets the Hearth rally; H H returns the camera to the start position exactly. Tests: 3 in `tests/game/layout.test.ts` (existing expectations unchanged).
- **B8 fixed (deviates from the patch).** Chromium/Firefox path as in the prototype: upsample, 4 edge strips and 4 corners into one staging canvas, one `blur()` draw. WebKit (no `ctx.filter`): the prototype's getImageData + RGBA box blur cost 10.6 ms per fog update in WebKit (3.5 ms readback + 7.1 ms blur, measured), 5-10 times a second. Fog and Blight are one colour each, so the fallback now upsamples the layer's 1-px alpha in JS (`upsampleAlpha`, clamped margin), runs a 3-pass alpha-only box blur (`boxBlurAlpha`, reused buffers) and puts the result with a cached ImageData. No canvas readback. Fog leak at 0:00 (raze d1 seed 21, border vs 4 tiles in): Chromium 31.1 vs 10.6 at HEAD → 11.5 vs 10.6; WebKit 10.6 vs 10.6 both. Frame time (renderStats, fog changing every 0.1 s, median / EMA): Chromium DPR 1 and 2 0.3 ms both builds; WebKit EMA 0.1-0.35 ms HEAD vs 0.24-0.49 ms fix. WebKit fog edges are now a smooth ramp instead of tile steps (screenshots b8-webkit-head/fix). Tests: `upsampleAlpha` and `boxBlurAlpha` in `tests/render/fogBlight.test.ts`.
- **B9 fixed (stacking, no merge).** `edgeArrows.stackLabels`: labels whose estimated boxes overlap (same width estimate as `labelShift`) drop below the earlier one, or rise above it when they would cross into the bottom panel; applied through a `--ly` CSS variable. Labels hide (chevrons stay) while a centre callout (LAST STAND, Wrath, Uprooted) or the end banner shows. Browser: Spent + Wave 3 + Raid on the bottom edge stack as three rows with no overlap; under a Last Stand callout all three are hidden, back after it. Tests: 3 in `tests/game/layout.test.ts`.
- **B10 fixed.** `keys.learnKeyLayout`: without the Keyboard API each unmodified single-character keydown records code → key; the first KeyZ→'y' / KeyY→'z' switches in a built-in QWERTZ table, KeyQ→'a' / KeyA→'q' an AZERTY table (both include Minus, Equal, Backquote, Comma, Period). The input layer calls it on every keydown and `InputHost.layoutChanged` invalidates the HUD. `hotkeyLabel` maps '−', '=', '`', '.', ',' and "− / =" parts; 'ß' stays 'ß'. The API map (Chromium) wins over learning. Browser (WebKit, no navigator.keyboard): a synthetic keydown {code:'KeyY', key:'z'} turns the Forgehall badges "Z X C" into "Y X C" and the speed tooltip into "ß / ´". Firefox could not be launched in this sandbox (profile error), same code path. Tests: 3 in `tests/game/keys.test.ts`.
- **B11 fixed.** The line telegraph (Wild Hunt, Thorn Lance) is a filled band pulsing around 35% opacity with a progress fill, a dark under-stroke plus a bright outline, and an arrowhead at the dash end. The `telegraph` event already had an audio cue (`boss_telegraph` in `src/audio/cues.ts`), so audio needed no change. Screenshot on Ashwood (elite d6 seed 1) read by eye.

## Deviations from the plan (also for the GDD writer)
- B8: WebKit fallback is the alpha-only JS path above, not the patch's RGBA box blur over a canvas readback (performance).
- B9: overlapping labels stack; Spent markers are not merged into "2 Spent".
- B7: `centerOn` keeps the top-bar clamp (opening view, H, Space unchanged); only pans and zooms use the band. The band is the tracker column, not the Horn (the Horn and coach change height and would make the clamp move).
- B2: three-cost labels wrap smaller instead of hiding the third cost. The run lane's app-level audio unlock also covers HUD taps; this lane only adds pointerup on the battle canvas.
- B4: added a "Wrath" callout on the phase change.

## GDD lines for S10
- A › click on an enemy unit or building attacks it directly; on the ground it is an attack-move (minimap A-clicks are always attack-moves).
- On touch: tap to select, tap the ground to move or attack, drag to pan, pinch to zoom. The HUD scales down to 0.6 on phones.
- The unit panel's Strong vs / Weak vs rows show a building icon for units that barely damage buildings (×0.2 Bowmen, ×0.4 Coursers) or tear them down (×4 Gatebreakers).
- The camera can pan the map's top rows out from under the objective tracker.
- The Spent ribbon names the wave only while it is current; Nests that share a direction are "near" / "far".
- Hotkey labels follow QWERTZ / AZERTY in every browser (Safari and Firefox learn it from the first keys pressed).
